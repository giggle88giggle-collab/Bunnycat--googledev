Privacy Policy

Entity: Bunny & Cat Studio (www.bunnycatstudio.online)
Effective Date: October 2026

Welcome to Bunny & Cat Studio. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information across all our applications published on the Google Play Store.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect directly identifying personal details (such as your full name, phone number, physical address, or contacts). We may collect:

  • Device & Analytics Data: IP address, device model, operating system version, and anonymous performance/crash diagnostics.
  • Gameplay Data: In-game achievements, unlocked levels, and high scores.

2. Third-Party Services

Our games may integrate trusted Google developer services:

  • Google Play Services: Cloud save, leaderboards, and authentication.
  • Google AdMob: Displaying contextual and family-safe advertisements.
  • Firebase Analytics: Measuring game performance and crash logs.

3. Children’s Privacy & Families Policy

We comply fully with COPPA and Google Play’s Families Policy. For all apps intended for children and families, we do not collect personal data from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.

4. User Data Deletion Request

Players can request the deletion of any stored gameplay statistics or identifiers at any time by emailing us at support@bunnycatstudio.online.
